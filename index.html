    How the prototype works

    Finds rebates a company earned but never claimed

    Suppliers pay a rebate once a year's purchases pass a threshold. When an acquired company bills on its own account, nobody counts it. The agent finds that, works out what is owed and by when, and you decide.

    The case it opens on
    The tipMeraki and Acacia, both owned by Cisco, bill on their own accounts and never count towards Cisco's rebate
    The agreement2 % of the year's purchases once they reach €50M
    What the agent finds€2.14M at stake; the first claim lapses in 31 days
    Its proposalOption A for €17,100 claims all of it, once you approve

    Five steps and the four figures they move

    The agent spots the tip, checks who belongs to the supplier group, lets an engine count what is owed, proposes three costed options and drafts what you approve. The four figures at the top say what that is worth.

    The four figures
    FigureWhat it measuresCisco case
    Rebate at stakeWhat the company loses if nobody claims, then what is left once you approve an option€2.14M, then €0
    Claim deadlineDays until the first claim lapses and the money is gone31 days
    Detection → decisionTime from the tip to your approval, measured on this page4 min on the talk track
    Session costModel fees for the agent's run: estimated here, measured in a live run≈ €0.14

    The company is fictional, the supplier groups are real

    GlobalCorp SE, its accounts, purchases and agreements are invented. Cisco, Microsoft and the other groups, their acquisitions and the dates are public facts. An engine computes every euro and day.

    Where everything comes from
    FictionalGlobalCorp SE: 3 sites, 161 supplier accounts, 1,320 orders and 8 rebate agreements, in 6 cases
    RealThe supplier groups and their acquisitions, the engine's arithmetic and, with your key, Claude itself
    Written for the replayThe agent's words and its web sources, marked as representative
    Nothing is sentClaims and changes stay drafts until you approve them, and even then go nowhere

    Show it in five minutes

    It plays by itself and stops where you act; Space pauses while you talk.

    1. Spot. It starts by itself; point at the four figures. “€2.14M in rebates at stake, and the first claim lapses in 31 days.” 1 min
    2. Check. Who really belongs to Cisco? “The tip names two acquired companies. The register finds a third, Splunk.” 1 min
    3. Calculate. The first count was wrong. “With the two from the tip, Cisco reaches €48M, short of €50M: €0. With Splunk, €52.4M: €1.05M owed for the closed year, €2.14M with this year's.” 1 min
    4. Propose. Press Approve option A. “For €17,100 we claim all of it. A person decides, in about 4 min.” 1 min
    5. Prepare. Press Approve both. “Two drafts wait for a person; nothing is sent. Live, this costs about €0.14.” 1 min
